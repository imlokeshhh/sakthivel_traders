About us

Built by riders,
stitched for the road.

Sakthivel Traders began as a small seat-cover workshop and grew into a trusted supplier of two-wheeler accessories across Tamil Nadu and beyond. Every cover is cut to the shape of a real bike seat or tank — not a generic template — so it sits tight, looks factory-fitted and lasts through monsoon and summer.

Today we serve everyday riders at our counter and online, and supply hundreds of accessory shops, service centres and distributors with consistent, dealer-priced stock.

      FAQ

      Good questions,
      straight answers.

      Can't find what you need? Ask us on WhatsApp.

      Will the cover fit my exact bike model?

      Yes. Each cover is patterned on a specific model. Tell us your bike's make, model and year (on the order or on WhatsApp) and we'll confirm the right fit before dispatch.

      How do I place an order?

      Add products to your cart on the Shop page and tap “Order on WhatsApp”. Your full order is sent to us in one message — we confirm stock, shipping and payment options right in the chat.

      What is the minimum order for wholesale?

      Wholesale pricing starts at 25 pieces, mixed across models and colours. Larger slabs unlock better rates — send the enquiry form for a quote within 24 hours.

      Do you deliver outside Tamil Nadu?

      Yes, we ship across India by tracked courier. Bulk orders can also go by transport to your nearest hub.

      Can I get a custom design with my name or logo?

      Absolutely. Choose a Custom Cover in the shop or message us your design idea. We share a preview before stitching.

      What if the cover doesn't fit?

      If a model-specific cover doesn't fit as described, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange an exchange.
